## Existing evidence and its boundary

- The [INV-039 stateful owner](stateful/inv_039_pending_loss_obligation_durability.rs)
  and `support/invariant_discovery.rs` own paired funding/accrual, shutdown,
  terminal-commit and partial-liquidation oracles. Their existing discovery
  fingerprints concern accrual ordering and stale cohorts. They do not establish
  rediscovery of pending residual attribution across mixed-role resolution.
- The closest generated attribution test is
  `v16_program_pending_resolved_history_generator_preserves_owner_debt` in
  [resolved_histories](cu/inv_039_pending_loss_resolved_histories.rs). Its
  input-derived oracle checks every close prefix, but generation changes only
  side, integral lots, price movement, early debtor settlement, and close/retry
  order. The two pairs always have distinct creditor/debtor portfolios, no
  funding or fees, and solvent debtors: at most 60,000 debt against deposits of
  180,000 and 250,000. It cannot generate a bankrupt close or a same-owner
  creditor/debtor composition.
- [mixed_role_resolution](cu/inv_039_mixed_role_resolution.rs) does check
  same-owner roles, residual booking, source support and exact owner payouts.
  Its debt inputs are 36,000/240,000 with fixed deposits and a fixed 20,000
  creditor-domain residual. Funding, fractional, expiry, split-debt and ADL
  children extend finite matrices. The separate
  [terminal-fee matrix](cu/inv_039_pending_loss_terminal_fees.rs) keeps creditors
  and debtors in distinct portfolios. These are useful conformance assertions,
  but no finding-blind counterexample establishes either held-out mapping.
- The [underfunded-receipt child](cu/inv_039_mixed_underfunded_receipt.rs)
  explicitly starts its economic oracle at a public checkpoint; it does not
  independently derive the preceding liquidation history's entitlement.
  INV-086's generated runner adds stock, encumbrance, position, rollback and
  exit checks, but those censuses reconcile observed state. They do not supply
  the missing historical per-owner debt oracle. A jointly erased leg and count,
  or an internally balanced reassignment between owners, need not violate a
  census or total-token conservation.

## Missing generic coverage

Row 419 needs generated pending residual/close-drift histories that retain each
owner's obligation until independently justified settlement, including resolution
and close ordering. Row 435 additionally needs the same portfolio to carry
creditor and debtor roles in different domains, with independent debt, collateral,
source support, fee/funding and terminal-conversion terms. A reference ledger
must derive owner/domain obligations from public inputs and retain them across
leg removal, residual booking, receipts and payouts, checking exact-once debt
discharge at each prefix. Aggregate conservation alone is insufficient.

Promotion requires a frozen finding-blind generator/oracle and reproducible
public economic failure evidence matching the held-out mechanism, with controls
showing detection of erased or reassigned debt. Another fixed row-shaped matrix,
a passing current-pin run, or a replacement PR's regression is not that evidence.
This audit adds no economic tests or discovery fingerprints.
